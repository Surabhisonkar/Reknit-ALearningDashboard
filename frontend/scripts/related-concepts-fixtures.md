## The cluster that should connect (the vision doc's own example)

**1. Back muscles** (folder: *Anatomy*)
> The main back muscles: erector spinae run along the spine and keep it upright; latissimus dorsi pull the arms down and back; rhomboids and middle trapezius squeeze the shoulder blades together. Weak or tired back muscles let the upper body round forward.

**2. Posture** (folder: *Everyday habits*)
> Good posture means the ears, shoulders and hips stack in a line. Slouching at a desk rounds the upper back and pushes the head forward, which strains the neck and lower back. Holding good posture depends on muscles that keep the spine upright and the shoulder blades back.

**3. Core stability** (folder: *Fitness*)
> The deep core (transverse abdominis, obliques, multifidus and pelvic floor) braces the trunk like a corset. A stable core protects the lower back when lifting and supports an upright spine when sitting or standing.

## Controls that should connect to none of the above

**4. Sourdough fermentation** (folder: *Cooking*)
> Sourdough rises because wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria in the starter ferment the flour's sugars, producing carbon dioxide for lift and acids for the sour taste. A longer, cooler ferment gives more flavour.

**5. Causes of the French Revolution** (folder: *History*)
> France in 1789 faced a debt crisis from wars, a rigid estate system that taxed commoners but not nobles, bread shortages after bad harvests, and Enlightenment ideas about rights, which together led to the fall of the monarchy.
